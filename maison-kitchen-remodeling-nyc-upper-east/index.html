About Maison Kitchen Remodeling NYC

We work out of 750 Lexington Ave and focus on one room: the kitchen. From pre-war apartments to modern co-ops, we help Upper East Side homeowners turn tight or dated kitchens into spaces that work properly. Message us on WhatsApp to start the conversation.

Maison Kitchen Remodeling NYC is a kitchen renovation team based at 750 Lexington Ave on the Upper East Side. We help homeowners plan, build and finish kitchens that suit the way they actually use them.

Manhattan kitchens come with their own challenges: limited space, older buildings and board requirements. We take those into account from the first conversation, so the plan is realistic and the result feels right.

If you are thinking about a new kitchen, message us on WhatsApp with a few photos and what you have in mind. We will take it from there.

  • Careful workClean installs, tidy job sites and finishes done properly.
  • Clear communicationYou know what is happening at each stage of your project.
  • Respect for your homeWe treat your apartment and building like they matter, because they do.
