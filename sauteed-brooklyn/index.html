About us

A neighbourhood kitchen on 13th Avenue

We're on 13th Avenue in Brooklyn, cooking food that's made when you order it and served hot. Come in for a sit-down meal, pick something up on the way home, or message us on WhatsApp and we'll get it ready.

Sauteed is a restaurant at 4006 13th Avenue in Brooklyn. Our name says how we like to cook: fresh ingredients, a hot pan, and food that reaches your plate while it's still sizzling.

We want to be the place you come back to, for a quick lunch, a relaxed dinner or a takeout order that's ready when you said you'd come. Everything here is about good food made simply and served with care.

Have a question about the menu, want to place an order or want to plan a meal for a group? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll help.

  • FreshWe cook to order so every dish comes out hot and tastes the way it should.
  • CarefulWe pay attention to the small things, from seasoning to how the plate looks.
  • WelcomingNeighbours, regulars and first-timers all get the same warm welcome.
