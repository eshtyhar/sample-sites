Restaurants

Waiting for your look.

THE TABLE: an animated sample-site template for every kind of New York restaurant. One Blender table for two in a made-up neighborhood restaurant, and the scroll is the evening at that table: it sets itself, the kitchen lights up, the camera looks down at the plate (their own dish photo drops onto it), a glass fills, the room goes to night and a RESERVED card lands, with Book a table, Order, Menu, Call and Directions on every screen. 14 desktop + 11 phone frames as a linked FigCraft prototype, one PDF, the renders in 3 moods with 4 scroll sequences, and build-spec.md.

These are the designer's screens (pictures), shown in scroll order. The real moving website is built only after you say yes.
