About us

About Forest Properties

We are a local real estate agency serving the Astoria neighbourhood. We help people find rental apartments, buy homes, and sell properties. We focus on making the process straightforward and transparent for everyone.

Forest Properties is a real estate agency serving the Astoria neighbourhood in Queens. We focus on helping people find homes to buy or rent. We work with clients who are looking to move into the area or are already here.

Our team is dedicated to providing clear information and support. We understand that buying or selling a home is a significant step. We aim to make the process as simple and stress-free as possible for everyone involved.

We believe in fair treatment for all clients. We help people from all backgrounds find homes in the Astoria community.

  • TransparencyWe provide clear and honest information about properties and processes.
  • RespectWe treat every client with dignity and respect.
  • CommunityWe are proud to serve the Astoria neighbourhood.
