About The Law Offices Of Jeffrey Kimmel

Legal questions can feel heavy, and the first step is often the hardest. At The Law Offices Of Jeffrey Kimmel, we start by listening, then explain your options in plain language. When you're ready, message us on WhatsApp or call the office.

The Law Offices Of Jeffrey Kimmel is a law office located at 270 Madison Avenue in Midtown Manhattan, in the middle of one of New York's busiest business districts.

We believe good legal help starts with a clear conversation. We take the time to understand your situation, explain how the process works and talk honestly about your options.

Whether you work nearby or are coming in from elsewhere in the city, the simplest way to begin is to message us on WhatsApp or call the office.
