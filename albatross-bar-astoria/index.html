About us

About Albatross Bar

We are a community-focused game store in Astoria dedicated to providing a welcoming space for gamers of all ages. Whether you are looking for the latest releases or a place to play, we are here to serve you.

Albatross Bar is a game store located in the heart of Astoria, New York. We are a local business dedicated to serving the gaming community of Queens and beyond.

Our goal is to provide a friendly and inclusive environment where gamers can find what they need and connect with others who share their interests. We strive to offer a wide variety of games and services to meet the needs of every player.

We are committed to being a reliable resource for the local community, offering a place to play, buy, and sell games. We look forward to welcoming you to our store.

  • CommunityBuilding a strong, supportive local gaming community.
  • PassionSharing our love for games and helping others discover new favorites.
  • ServiceProviding friendly and knowledgeable service to every customer.
