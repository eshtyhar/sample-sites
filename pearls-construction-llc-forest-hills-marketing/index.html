04 · Content ideas for Pearls Construction LLC

What we'd post, and how

Construction Process Reel

A Reel showing a time-lapse of a site being prepared. Start with a raw site and show the progress of pouring concrete or framing. This visualizes the 'Construction Consultancy Services' and 'Site Safety' mentioned on your site.

Team and Safety Carousel

A Carousel post for Instagram. Slide 1: A photo of your team on site. Slide 2: A photo showing safety gear or a safety meeting. Slide 3: A photo of a completed project. This builds trust and shows your commitment to quality.

Neighborhood Project Highlight

A Story or Reel featuring a project near Forest Hills. Show the before and after of a renovation or build. This targets your local audience and highlights your presence in the Queens Blvd area.
