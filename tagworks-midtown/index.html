About us

Pet care with a Midtown heart

City pets need space, routine and people who pay attention. At TagWorks in Midtown, we give every dog and cat patient, personal care so busy owners can relax. Message us on WhatsApp to tell us about your pet.

TagWorks is a pet care business in Midtown Manhattan for owners who want their pets looked after by people who care. We know city life can be busy, loud and short on space, for pets and for their people.

Every pet is different. Some are confident and social, others need a little more time and reassurance. We listen to what you tell us about your pet and treat them with patience and respect.

If you live or work in Midtown, we would love to meet you and your pet. Message us on WhatsApp to ask a question or arrange a visit.
