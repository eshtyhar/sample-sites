About us

About The Gamers Club

We are a local game store in Midtown, New York, serving the West Hempstead community. We offer a wide selection of new and used games, consoles, and collectible cards for all ages and interests.

The Gamers Club is a game store located in Midtown, New York. We are dedicated to providing a welcoming space for gamers in West Hempstead and the surrounding areas.

Our store offers a diverse inventory of new and used games, consoles, and trading cards. We aim to be a hub for the local gaming community.

Whether you are looking for the latest release, a rare collectible, or just a place to meet fellow players, we invite you to visit us.

  • CommunityWe believe in building a welcoming and inclusive environment for all gamers.
  • PassionOur team shares a genuine love for gaming and is passionate about helping you find what you need.
  • ServiceWe provide friendly and knowledgeable service to ensure a great shopping experience.
