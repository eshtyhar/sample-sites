About us

About Brooklyn Landscaping & Sprinkler System

Brooklyn Landscaping & Sprinkler System works on yards, gardens and irrigation from our base on Avenue T. Whether you need a tidy lawn, new plantings or a sprinkler system that waters only where it should, we plan the work with you and do it carefully. Send us a message on WhatsApp and tell us what your outdoor space needs.

Brooklyn Landscaping & Sprinkler System is based at 203 Avenue T and works on outdoor spaces across Brooklyn. We handle the planting, the lawn and the watering, so your yard looks good and stays that way.

Every property is different. A narrow row-house garden needs different planning than a corner lot, and a sprinkler system should match the layout, the sun and the soil. We look at your space, listen to what you want, and plan the work around it.

If you're thinking about a new lawn, a cleanup, or a sprinkler system that finally works properly, message us on WhatsApp and let's talk it through.

  • Careful workClean lines, healthy plants and irrigation set up to do its job.
  • Straight talkWe explain what your yard needs and why, in plain terms.
  • Respect for your propertyWe work tidily and leave the site clean when we're done.
