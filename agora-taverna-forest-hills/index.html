About us

A taverna for the neighbourhood

On Austin Street in the middle of Forest Hills, Agora Taverna is a place to slow down, share plates and stay a while. Guests rate us 4.4 on Google, and we'd love to see you at the table. Message us on WhatsApp to book or ask a question.

In Greece, a taverna is where people gather to eat well, talk for hours and feel at home. Agora Taverna brings that same spirit to Austin Street in Forest Hills.

The cooking is generous and made for sharing, and the room is relaxed. Come for a quick weeknight dinner or a long weekend meal, and you'll get the same warm welcome.

Planning a visit, a group dinner or a takeout order? Message us on WhatsApp or call 718-793-7300 and we'll take care of you.

  • GenerosityPlates that fill the table and are meant to be shared.
  • CommunityA neighbourhood table where Forest Hills comes together.
