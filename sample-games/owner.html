For the owner

How this sitebrings people in.

In plain words: six ways it turns people looking on their phone into bookings, sign-ups, calls and walk-ins.

No numbers, no promises: what it does, and why that helps.

  1. 01

    Bookings, even when you're busy

    Tables, consoles, the arcade and rooms can be booked from a phone, day or night. People pick a slot instead of calling in your busiest hour.

  2. 02

    The big groups find you

    Birthdays, parties and team nights come in through one short form with the group size, so you can say yes to the bookings that fill the room.

  3. 03

    Game nights fill up

    Your tournaments, league and learn-to-play nights sit on every screen. Players see what's on, sign up, and come back next week.

  4. 04

    More walk-ins from nearby

    Open now, hours, the map and Directions are one tap away. Someone looking for something to do tonight sees you are open and how to get there.

  5. 05

    Stock questions turn into sales

    “Check stock” and Call are always on screen. People ask if the game, the cards or the comic is in, then come in to buy it.

  6. 06

    New groups trust you first

    Your own photos of the tables and rooms and your Google reviews show a real place, so a group that has never been feels safe booking you.
