About us

An Italian table in Midtown

Tucked into 54 West 55th Street, Il Corso is a calm place to sit down in the middle of Midtown. Come for a working lunch, a dinner before a show, or an evening with friends. Guests rate us 4.6 on Google, and we'd be glad to welcome you.

Il Corso is at 54 West 55th Street, in the heart of Midtown Manhattan. The neighbourhood is busy with offices, shops and theatres, and we're a place to slow down and enjoy a proper meal.

The idea is simple: honest Italian cooking, friendly service and a room where you can talk without raising your voice. Join us for a quick lunch or stay for a long dinner.

To plan a visit, ask about a dish or arrange a table for a group, message us on WhatsApp or call +1 212-957-1500.
