Accessibility, privacy and terms

Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use this site: it works with a keyboard and a screen reader, the words have strong contrast, and nothing moves if your device asks for less motion.

Something hard to use? Call us at (718) 555-0163 and we will help, and fix it.

Privacy

When you book or ask for an estimate, we use your name, phone and photos only to reply to you and to work on your car. We never sell them or share them with anyone else, except your insurer if you ask us to handle a claim.

This site does not use tracking cookies. Ask us at (718) 555-0163 to see or delete what we hold about you.

Terms

A booking is not confirmed until we text or call you to confirm the day.

An estimate from photos is a first price. We give you a written estimate before any work starts, and we never do extra work without your OK.
