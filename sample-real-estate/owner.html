For the owner

How this site brings in clients.

In plain words: six ways it turns people browsing on their phone into viewings, valuation requests, management proposals and calls.

No numbers, no promises: what it does, and why that helps.

  • 01

    Sellers come to you first

    “What's my home worth?” sits on every screen. Owners thinking of selling ask you for a price, and that conversation is how listings start.

  • 02

    Viewings without the back-and-forth

    Every listing has a “Book a viewing” button. People pick a day and a time on their phone, and the request reaches you with their name and number.

  • 03

    Building owners ask for a proposal

    A short form for owners: the address, how many units, what kind of building. It lands in your inbox, ready for a call.

  • 04

    Your listings, easy to search on a phone

    Area, beds and price, with your own photos and floor plans. People see the place, then book it, call or text you from the same page.

  • 05

    Found by people searching your area

    A page for each neighborhood you work in, in your own words, so people looking for a home there can find you.

  • 06

    People pick a person

    Your team, your licence and your Google reviews sit up front, with one-tap call and text. A new client knows who they'll talk to.
