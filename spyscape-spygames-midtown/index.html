About us

About SpyScape

We are a game store and experiential attraction in Midtown, New York. Our space blends interactive exhibits with a full retail shop, offering a place to play, learn, and explore.

SpyScape is a unique attraction in Midtown, New York. Our mission is to create an immersive environment where visitors can step into the world of espionage and intelligence. We combine interactive exhibits with a full retail shop to offer a complete entertainment experience.

Our space is designed to be engaging and educational. Visitors can test their skills in our various challenges, browse our selection of games, and learn about the history of intelligence. We strive to provide a fun and memorable experience for all ages.

  • ImmersiveCreating experiences that transport you to another world.
  • InteractiveEncouraging visitors to actively participate and solve challenges.
  • CommunityBuilding a space where people can connect and play together.

What can I do at SpyScape?

You can explore our interactive exhibits, play games in our shop, and challenge yourself with puzzles and simulations. You can also purchase games and accessories.

Do you sell games and consoles?

Yes, we have a retail shop with a selection of new and used games, consoles, and related merchandise.

Is there an entry fee?

Yes, entry to our interactive exhibits is a ticketed experience. You can message us on WhatsApp for pricing and details.

Do you host game nights?
