Accessibility, privacy and terms

Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use this site: it works with a keyboard and a screen reader, the words have strong contrast, and nothing moves if your device asks for less motion.

The salon is at street level with a step-free door. Something hard to use, on the site or in the salon? Call us at (718) 555-0163 and we will help.

Privacy

When you book, we use your name and mobile only to confirm your time and send you a reminder. We never sell them or share them with anyone else.

This site does not use tracking cookies. Ask us at (718) 555-0163 to see or delete what we hold about you.

Terms

Your time is held when we text you to confirm it. Please give us 24 hours' notice to change or cancel, so someone else can have the time.

The prices on the menu are what you pay. Colour and microblading start at the price shown: we tell you the full price before we begin. Gift cards: any amount, at the front desk or by phone.

NYS licences on display at the front desk. Wax, lash and microblading: a short consent form at your first visit. Microblading is 18+ with photo ID.
