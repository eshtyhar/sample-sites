About us

A Korean table in Park Slope

Haenyeo is a neighbourhood restaurant at 239 5th Ave in Park Slope, where Korean flavours meet an easygoing Brooklyn table. Come in for a relaxed weeknight meal, a long dinner with friends or something to share. Guests on Google rate us 4.5, and we'd love to welcome you next.

Haenyeo takes its name from the haenyeo, the women divers of Korea's coast, known for their strength and their deep connection to the sea. That spirit of care and respect for good ingredients shapes the food we put on the table.

Our home is 239 5th Ave, on one of Park Slope's busiest and friendliest stretches. We cook food that feels both comforting and lively, and our room suits an easy weeknight bite as well as a full evening out.

Want to book a table, ask about the menu or plan a group dinner? Message us on WhatsApp or call +1 718-213-2290.
