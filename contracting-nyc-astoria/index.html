Contracting NYC is a construction company based in Astoria, Queens. We take on renovation, repair and build-out work for homeowners, landlords and business owners who want the job done properly.

Building work in New York has its own challenges: older buildings, tight spaces, busy streets and neighbours close by. We plan around all of that so the work moves forward and your day-to-day life is disrupted as little as possible.

Our 4.9 Google rating comes from clients who trusted us with their spaces. If you have a project in mind, big or small, message us on WhatsApp and tell us about it.
