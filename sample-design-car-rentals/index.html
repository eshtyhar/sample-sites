Car rentals

Old version. You asked for real cars; the remake is in line.

WHERE TO?: an animated sample-site template for New York car rentals (everyday, exotic and luxury, vans, chauffeur). One unbadged Blender car on a city rooftop parking deck, and the scroll is the rental day: the light goes from morning to golden hour to night and back to morning (pick up, keys, choose, drive, arrive, night, return), with dates + car + Reserve, Call and WhatsApp on every screen. 15 desktop + 12 phone frames as a linked FigCraft prototype, one PDF, the Blender renders in 3 moods with the day scroll sequences, and build-spec.md.

These are the designer's screens (pictures), shown in scroll order. The real moving website is built only after you say yes.
