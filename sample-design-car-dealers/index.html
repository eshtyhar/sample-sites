Car dealers

New: remade with real cars. Waiting for your yes.

THE GATE, redone with real cars: an animated sample-site template for New York car dealers. A roll-down gate rises as you scroll onto a showroom turntable with six photo-real cars a NY dealer sells (Mercedes G-Class, Porsche 911, Honda Civic, Land Rover Defender, Tesla Model 3, Dodge Charger); the Choose step switches between them. 16 desktop + 11 phone frames as a linked FigCraft prototype, one PDF, the Blender renders and build-spec.md.

These are the designer's screens (pictures), shown in scroll order. The real moving website is built only after you say yes.
