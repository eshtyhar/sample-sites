About Big City Paving & Construction

We're a paving and general construction company based at 299 Manhattan St on Staten Island. From a cracked driveway to a new patio or sidewalk, we focus on careful prep, clean work and surfaces built to last. Send us a few photos on WhatsApp and we'll take it from there.

Big City Paving & Construction is a Staten Island company working on paving, concrete, masonry and general construction projects for homeowners and businesses. Our base is at 299 Manhattan St, close to the streets and driveways we work on.

We believe good work comes down to the basics: a proper look at the site, honest advice on what it needs, solid preparation and a tidy finish. Whether the job is a small repair or a full replacement, we give it the same attention.

If you have a project in mind, the easiest way to start is to message us on WhatsApp with a few photos and a short description. We'll get back to you to talk it through.

  • Do it rightWe'd rather spend time on preparation than come back to fix shortcuts.
  • Respect the propertyWe work carefully around your home or business and leave the site clean.
  • Straight talkPlain answers about what your project needs and how we'll approach it.
