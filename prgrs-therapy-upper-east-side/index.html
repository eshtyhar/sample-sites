About us

A calm practice on 3rd Avenue

PRGRS Therapy is a small practice at 1651 3rd Ave, Room 205, in the heart of the Upper East Side. Each visit is unhurried, private and built around you. To ask a question or book a visit, message us on WhatsApp.

PRGRS Therapy is on the second floor of 1651 3rd Ave, Room 205, on the Upper East Side of Manhattan. It is a quiet place to step away from the city's pace and focus on your visit.

Our approach is simple. We listen, explain things clearly and plan each visit around the person in the room. We will never rush you through an appointment.

If you live or work nearby and are thinking about booking, message us on WhatsApp. We are happy to answer questions about scheduling and how visits work.

  • RespectEvery person is treated with patience, privacy and care.
  • ClarityPlain explanations so you always know what happens next.
  • ConsistencySteady, regular visits at a pace that suits you.
