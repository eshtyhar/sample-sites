03 · Content ideas for Nunez Construction Inc.

What we'd post, and how

Kitchen, bath and basement before-and-after

Short reels that open on the old space and cut to the finished remodel, using clips your crew films on kitchen, bathroom and basement jobs. A quick text overlay names the room and the work done.

How we do it: roofing, siding, waterproofing

A step-by-step reel or carousel following one exterior job from start to finish, showing the prep, the materials and the final result. It explains in plain words why each step matters for the home.

What Queens homeowners say

A carousel series featuring quotes from your Google reviews, rated 4.8 from 251 reviews, each paired with a photo of the type of work mentioned. It's easy social proof for anyone in Flushing and Queens comparing contractors.

Every post designed and edited by us, in your look - you approve the plan, we do the rest.
