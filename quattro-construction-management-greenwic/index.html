About us

About Quattro Construction Management

Quattro Construction Management works on projects throughout Greenwich Village. We handle the logistics and management so your project moves forward with clear communication.

Quattro Construction Management is a New York-based firm focused on managing construction projects. We work with property owners, developers, and managers to oversee the execution of their projects.

Our approach is to provide clear, organized management. We coordinate the various trades and suppliers required to complete a project, ensuring that the work is done efficiently and according to the agreed-upon plans.

We serve clients in Greenwich Village and the surrounding Manhattan neighborhoods, handling projects of various sizes.

  • ReliabilityWe are committed to meeting deadlines and communicating consistently.
  • ProfessionalismWe conduct all work with a high standard of conduct and quality.
  • TransparencyWe provide clear information regarding project status and costs.
