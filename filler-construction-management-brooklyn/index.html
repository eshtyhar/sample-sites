About us

About Filler Construction Management

We're based at 7420 Ridge Blvd in Brooklyn and work with homeowners, landlords and businesses who want their project run in an organised way. We keep the schedule, the trades and the details moving together so you always know where things stand. Message us on WhatsApp to get started.

Filler Construction Management is a Brooklyn construction company working out of 7420 Ridge Blvd. We help clients take a building or renovation project from an idea to a finished space, with the planning and coordination that keeps it on course.

Good construction management comes down to keeping people informed and keeping the many moving parts working together. We focus on clear scopes, sensible schedules and steady supervision on site, so the work gets done properly and you know what's happening at each stage.

If you're planning work on a home, a rental property or a commercial space in Brooklyn, message us on WhatsApp and tell us about it. We'll talk through what you have in mind and the next steps.

  • ClarityClear scopes, clear schedules and plain answers to your questions.
  • CareWe treat your home or business with respect, on site and off.
  • Follow-throughWe see the details through to the finish, not just the big pieces.
