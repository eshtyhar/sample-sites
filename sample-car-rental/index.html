Questions people ask

How old do I have to be?

21 for everyday cars, 25 for SUVs and luxury, 30 for the 911. You need a full licence held for at least a year.

Is there a deposit?

Yes, a hold on your card: $300 for everyday cars, $1,500 for exotics. It's released when the car comes back.

Can you deliver the car?

Yes, across Brooklyn and Lower Manhattan for $49 each way. Pick "Deliver to me" when you reserve.

Do I need my own insurance?

Your own policy works if it covers rentals. If not, add ours from $25 a day.
