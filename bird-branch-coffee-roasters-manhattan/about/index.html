About us

A coffee bar for the neighbourhood

Bird & Branch Coffee Roasters pours carefully made coffee in the middle of Manhattan. Stop in for your morning cup or a calm break between the busy streets.

Bird & Branch Coffee Roasters is a coffee shop on West 45th Street in Manhattan. We serve people who want a good cup without any fuss, from early commuters to anyone spending the afternoon nearby.

Good coffee depends on lots of small details: fresh beans, a careful grind, the right water and a steady hand behind the bar. We pay attention to all of them so your cup tastes the way it should.

Whether you're a regular or it's your first visit, come in, say hello and find your favourite. For questions, message us on WhatsApp.
