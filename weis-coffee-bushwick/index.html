About Us

We are a cafe in Bushwick serving quality coffee and simple food. We focus on the coffee itself, providing a space to pause in the neighbourhood.

Weis Coffee is a cafe located in Bushwick. We are committed to serving coffee that is brewed with care. Our space is designed to be a comfortable place for you to spend time.

We believe in the simple pleasure of a good coffee. We source our beans and prepare them with attention to detail. Our goal is to provide a consistent experience for our guests.
