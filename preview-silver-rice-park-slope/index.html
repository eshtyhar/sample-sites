About us

A sushi roll shop in Brooklyn

Silver Rice is a sushi roll shop on Park Place in Brooklyn. Our idea is simple: make every roll in the shop, every day, with ingredients we've chosen carefully.

Alongside our rolls we serve Rice Cups, a Silver Rice speciality. Come in to try them, order online for pickup or delivery, or book a table on Resy.

  • Made dailyRolls are made fresh in the shop each day, not ahead of time.
  • Care in every rollWe choose our ingredients carefully and make each roll by hand.
  • Made for the neighbourhoodA friendly local spot for a quick lunch, dinner or takeout.

How do I order online?

Tap "Order online" to order through our DoorDash ordering page. You can also call the shop at +1 718-398-8100.

Can I reserve a table?

Yes, you can book a table through Resy. For anything else, give us a call.

What are Rice Cups?

Rice Cups are a Silver Rice speciality. Call or stop by and we'll tell you about today's choices.

Do you have vegetarian options or handle allergies?

We have vegetable rolls. If you have an allergy, please call before you order so we can talk you through the menu.

Do you sell gift cards?

Yes, Silver Rice gift cards are available. Call the shop or check our website for details.
