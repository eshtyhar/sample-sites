About us

A neighbourhood bakery in Long Island City

WildFlour artisanal cakes and Bakery is a neighbourhood spot for anyone in Long Island City who wants something baked with care. Stop in for a slice and a coffee, or plan a cake for a day that matters. Our Google rating of 4.6 comes from neighbours who keep coming back.

WildFlour artisanal cakes and Bakery sits on 44th Ave in Long Island City. It's a place for good cake, fresh bakes and a coffee break that feels a little slower than the rest of the day.

We think a good bakery should feel personal. That means baking things properly, keeping the counter inviting and taking care with every order, from one slice to a whole cake for a celebration.

If you're planning something special or just want to know what's fresh, the easiest way to reach us is to message us on WhatsApp.

  • Care in every bakeEach cake and pastry gets proper time and attention.
  • Neighbourly serviceFriendly, straightforward help whether you're a regular or new to LIC.
  • Made to be sharedWe bake for birthdays, gatherings and ordinary days that deserve something sweet.
