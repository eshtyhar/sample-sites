About us

A neighbourhood bakery in Astoria

Gian Piero Bakery is a neighbourhood bakery and cafe at 44-17 30th Ave., where people stop in for a coffee, a sweet and a moment to sit down. Our neighbours have rated us 4.7 on Google, and we work to keep it that way every day. Come by, or message us on WhatsApp with any question.

Gian Piero Bakery sits at 44-17 30th Ave., part of the daily routine for many people in Astoria. It is the kind of place you stop for a coffee on the way somewhere, or pick up a box of sweets before visiting family.

We keep it simple: fresh baked goods, coffee made properly and a warm welcome at the counter. Whether you sit down for a while or grab something to go, we want every visit to feel easy.

Planning a celebration or a gathering? Message us on WhatsApp and we will help you put together the right order.
