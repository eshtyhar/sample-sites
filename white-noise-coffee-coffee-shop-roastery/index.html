About us

A roastery and coffee shop on Manhattan's West Side

We roast our own coffee and pour it at 829 11th Avenue, a few steps from the river side of Midtown. Stop in for a morning espresso, a slow pour-over or a bag of beans to take home. Questions or a larger order? Message us on WhatsApp.

White Noise Coffee is a coffee shop and roastery at 829 11th Avenue. We roast our coffee and serve it at the bar, so the beans you drink are the beans we know best.

The idea is simple: good coffee, made properly, in a calm spot where you can stop for a minute or grab a cup and go. Whether you live nearby, work in Midtown or are passing through, you're welcome here.

Want to order beans, plan coffee for a group or just ask a question? Message us on WhatsApp or give us a call.

  • FreshnessRoasting in-house means coffee that tastes the way it should.
  • ConsistencyThe same care in the first cup of the morning and the last.
  • HospitalityA friendly welcome for regulars and first-timers alike.
