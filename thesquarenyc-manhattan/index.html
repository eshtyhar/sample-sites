About us

About Thesquare.NYC

Building in Manhattan means tight spaces, busy streets and neighbours on every side. Thesquare.NYC works out of East 39th Street and plans each job around those conditions, so the work gets done cleanly and with as little disruption as possible. Tell us what you have in mind and we'll take it from there.

Thesquare.NYC is a construction business at 10 E 39th St in Manhattan. We take on renovation and building work for homes and businesses across the borough, with a focus on doing each job carefully and keeping our clients informed throughout.

Construction in Manhattan comes with its own set of challenges: building management requirements, limited access, shared walls and neighbours close by. We plan around those realities from the first conversation so the project runs as smoothly as it can.

Whether you're updating a single room or reworking an entire space, the first step is simple. Message us on WhatsApp, tell us about the project, and we'll get back to you to talk it through.

  • Care in the detailsGood work shows in the finishes, so we treat every edge, joint and surface with attention.
  • Honest communicationStraight answers about scope, timing and next steps, from the first message onward.
  • Respect for your spaceWe work cleanly and considerately in your home, your business and your building.
