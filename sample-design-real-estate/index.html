Real estate

Old version. The remake (Empire State Building, then into the apartment) is in line.

COME ON UP: an animated sample-site template for every New York real-estate agent, broker, rental broker, property manager and mortgage broker. One Blender architectural model of a made-up New York corner at twilight (a white brownstone with furnished rooms beside a pre-war apartment building with a water tower), and the scroll rides it like an elevator: the windows light up, the floors lift apart, the roof comes off into a floor plan, a real key ring lands by the stoop and the door opens, with Search, Book a viewing, What's my home worth, Ask for a proposal and Call on every screen. 17 desktop + 13 phone frames as a linked FigCraft prototype, one PDF, the renders in 3 moods with 3 scroll sequences, and build-spec.md.

These are the designer's screens (pictures), shown in scroll order. The real moving website is built only after you say yes.
