About us

A neighbourhood restaurant in Midtown

Tucked away at 244 West 56th Street, Joe G is a relaxed place to slow down in the middle of Midtown. Come for a quiet lunch, a dinner with friends or a meal before a night out. Guests rate us 4.5 on Google, and we'd be glad to welcome you.

Joe G Restaurant sits at 244 West 56th Street, a short walk from some of Midtown's busiest corners. Inside, the pace slows down. We want every guest to feel at home, whether you're a regular or just passing through.

We focus on what matters most in a good restaurant: food made with care, friendly service and a room where you can relax. Guests have given us a 4.5 rating on Google, and we work to earn it every time you sit down.

Want to book a table, ask about the menu or plan a meal for a group? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll take it from there.
