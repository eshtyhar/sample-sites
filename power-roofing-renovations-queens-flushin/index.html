About us

Roofing and renovations, done with care

Power Roofing Renovations Queens works from Sanford Avenue in Flushing, looking after the roofs and homes of our neighbours. Whether it's a leak after a storm or a renovation you've been planning for a while, we explain the job in plain terms. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll talk it through.

Power Roofing Renovations Queens is a roofing and renovation company based in Flushing. We work on the roofs, gutters, exteriors and interiors of homes and buildings in the neighbourhood and across Queens.

A good roofing job starts with a proper look at the problem. We check what's actually going on, explain your options in plain language, and then do the work neatly so it lasts through New York winters and summer storms.

Have a leak, an ageing roof or a renovation in mind? Message us on WhatsApp with a few photos and tell us what you need.

  • Honest assessmentsWe tell you what your roof or home actually needs, nothing more.
  • Careful workmanshipClean, tidy work that's done right the first time.
  • Respect for your homeWe protect your property and clean up when the job is done.
