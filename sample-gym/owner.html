For the owner

How this sitefills your classes.

In plain words: six ways it turns people searching on their phone into booked first classes, tours, calls and walk-ins.

No numbers, no promises: what it does, and why that helps.

  1. 01

    First classes booked from the phone

    'Book a first class' sits on every screen, so the moment someone decides, they're on your list. People look for a class on their phone, often late at night, and don't wait for a reply.

  2. 02

    Fuller classes

    Your real schedule, one tap away, with a Book button on each class. People see a time that fits and take the spot, instead of messaging to ask what's on.

  3. 03

    More calls and tours

    Call and 'Book a tour' are on every screen. The people who want to see the room first can, and the walk-in becomes a member.

  4. 04

    Beginners feel welcome

    The first-visit steps, what to bring and your coaches' real photos answer "is this place for someone like me?" before they walk in.

  5. 05

    Fewer "how much?" messages

    Your memberships, packs and drop-in prices in plain words, so people who are ready can just book.

  6. 06

    Easy to find, easy to come back

    Hours, the map, the subway and which door, plus your Google reviews: new people find you, and members bring a friend.
