Accessibility, privacy and terms

Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use this site: it works with a keyboard and a screen reader, the words have strong contrast, and nothing moves if your device asks for less motion.

Something hard to use, on the site or in the studio? Call us at (718) 555-0147 and we will help, and fix it.

Privacy

When you book, we use your name and mobile only to confirm your class and to text you about it. We never sell them or share them with anyone else.

This site does not use tracking cookies. Ask us at (718) 555-0147 to see or delete what we hold about you.

Terms

A booking holds your spot until the class starts. Please cancel at least 2 hours before, from the text we send you or by calling (718) 555-0147, so someone on the waitlist can have it.

Monthly memberships renew each month until you cancel, with 30 days' notice. Class packs can't be refunded, but you can give unused classes to a friend.

Before your first class you sign a short waiver at the front desk. Prices can change: we always tell you the price before you pay.
