About us

A neighbourhood bakery on the Grand Concourse

We're a neighbourhood bakery and cafe at 1375 Grand Concourse, baking for Hunts Point and the wider Bronx. Come in for something warm in the morning, a coffee break in the afternoon, or a box of treats to share. To place an order or ask what's fresh today, message us on WhatsApp.

JIASÚ BAKERY is a bakery and cafe at 1375 Grand Concourse in the Bronx, serving Hunts Point and the surrounding neighbourhoods. We're the kind of place you stop at on your way somewhere, and sometimes stay a little longer than you planned.

We want simple things done well: fresh bread, pastries that taste the way they should, and a good cup of coffee at a counter where people know your face. Whether you're grabbing breakfast, picking up something sweet for the family or ordering for a gathering, we'll take care of you.

Want to know what's coming out of the oven or set something aside? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll get back to you.

  • Freshness firstWe bake regularly so what you take home tastes like it just came out of the oven.
  • Care in every bakeSmall details matter, from the shape of a roll to how the coffee is pulled.
  • Rooted in the BronxWe bake for our neighbours and want to be a dependable part of their day.
