About us

About Chiropractic in the City

Our practice sits on the 8th floor at 37 W 26th St, a short walk from the busiest parts of Midtown. Dr. Diana Salazar takes time with every patient and keeps each visit clear, calm and unhurried. When you're ready, message us on WhatsApp to book.

Chiropractic in the City is the practice of Dr. Diana Salazar, on the 8th floor at 37 W 26th St in Midtown Manhattan. The practice is meant to feel personal: a calm room, a clear conversation and time to get to know each patient.

Midtown runs at a fast pace, so we try to make care simple to fit in. You can book easily, the location is central and every visit has a clear plan. Whether you're coming before work, on a lunch break or at the end of the day, just message us on WhatsApp to arrange a time.

  • RespectEvery patient is listened to and treated with care and courtesy.
  • ClarityPlain explanations, so you always know what to expect from each visit.
  • ConsistencyReliable, friendly care from the same practitioner, visit after visit.
