For the owner

How this sitebrings in clients.

In plain words: six ways it turns dog owners looking on their phone into booked grooms and stays, calls and walk-ins.

No numbers, no promises: what it does, and why that helps.

  1. 01

    Grooms and stays get booked after hours

    “Book a groom or stay” is on every screen. Owners plan their dog's week at night and on weekends, while you are busy with the dogs in front of you, and you confirm in the morning.

  2. 02

    Calls and directions in one tap

    “Call us” and Directions sit on every screen, so a first-timer standing on the corner finds your door, and a regular running late reaches you.

  3. 03

    New dogs arrive ready

    The new dog form and vaccine records come in before the first drop-off, so check-in at the counter is quick.

  4. 04

    Packages, one tap away

    If you sell day packs, class packs or groom plans, they sit on every screen with what's in them, so owners can pick one before they ask at the counter.

  5. 05

    Found by pet owners nearby

    Your neighborhood and services in plain words, so people searching for a groomer, daycare or trainer near them can find you. Directions are one tap.

  6. 06

    People trust a face

    Your own team photos with dogs and your Google reviews up front. A new owner knows who will look after their dog.
