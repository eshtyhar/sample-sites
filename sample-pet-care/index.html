Services

What we help with.

Grooming, daycare, training and the shop in plain words: what it is, which pets, and how to book it.

  • Full groom

    Bath, brush-out, haircut, nails and ears, finished with a warm dry on the table. One dog at a time.

    ForDogs of every size

  • Bath + tidy

    A warm bath in the raised tub, a blow-dry, nails, and a tidy round the face, feet and tail.

    ForDogs between full grooms

  • Nails and tidy-ups

    Nails clipped and filed, a quick brush-out and a trim round the eyes. About 20 minutes.

    ForDogs · cats

  • Daycare

    A full or half day in the gated play room: rubber floor, beds, water, toys and a nap after lunch.

    ForDogs over 16 weeks, after a trial day

  • Training

    Puppy classes, manners and leash walking, in small groups or one-to-one with our trainer.

    ForPuppies · grown dogs

  • The shop

    Food, treats, toys, leashes and collars by the counter. Pick them up when you collect your dog.

    ForDogs · cats
