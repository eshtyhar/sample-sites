About us

A caring, relaxed dental office in Flushing

The dental practice of Evonne Hwang, DDS, also known as H&N Dental Care, is on Main Street in Flushing, Queens. Our goal is simple: to help you take good care of your teeth in a caring, relaxed and friendly place.

We offer general check-ups and cleanings as well as tooth-colored restorations, composite inlays and onlays, veneers, crowns and bridges. We talk through every option in plain words so you can make your own decisions.

Patients come to us from across Flushing and nearby neighborhoods like Auburndale, Murray Hill, Fresh Meadows, Bay Terrace and Corona. Our website has information in both English and Chinese.

  • ComfortA calm, friendly visit matters as much as the care itself.
  • ClarityPlain explanations and your questions answered before any treatment.
  • CommunityA neighborhood office on Main Street, here for Flushing families.
