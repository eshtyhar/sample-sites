About us

Island cooking in the heart of the Bronx

J&M Caribbean Seafood brings bold island cooking to 1387 Webster Ave in the Bronx. Come hungry for well-seasoned seafood, hearty sides and the warm flavours of home. To check today's menu or place an order, message us on WhatsApp.

J&M Caribbean Seafood is a neighbourhood spot on Webster Ave serving Caribbean seafood cooked the way people remember it: well seasoned, generously spiced and full of flavour.

Whether you're grabbing a plate on the way home or ordering for the family, the idea is simple. Good seafood, classic island sides and food that makes you feel at home in Fordham.
