About One Manhattan Dental

We're a dental practice in Suite 1001 at 575 Madison Avenue, easy to reach from the Upper East Side and Midtown. Our patients rate us 5 stars across 273 Google reviews, and we try to make every visit calm and unhurried. Message us on WhatsApp to find a time that suits you.

One Manhattan Dental is a dental practice at 575 Madison Avenue, Suite 1001, serving patients from the Upper East Side, Midtown and across Manhattan. We want a dental visit to feel calm, straightforward and personal.

We take time to listen, explain things clearly and plan your care with you. Whether you're coming in for a routine check-up or bringing the family, we want you to feel at ease from the moment you arrive.

Our patients have given us a 5-star rating across 273 Google reviews, and we work to earn that trust at every visit. To book a visit, message us on WhatsApp or call 212-223-3632.
