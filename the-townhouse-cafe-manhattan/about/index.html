About us

A neighbourhood cafe on Lexington Avenue

The Townhouse Cafe serves good coffee and fresh bites on Lexington Avenue in Manhattan. Our guests rate us 4.9 on Google. Come in for a quick cup or stay a while.

The Townhouse Cafe is a local coffee spot on Lexington Avenue in Manhattan. We keep things simple: good drinks, fresh food and a welcoming place to spend a few minutes or a few hours.

Some people come in for their usual order on the way to work. Others sit down with a laptop, a book or a friend. Whatever brings you in, we want you to feel at home.

Our 4.9 rating on Google comes from the people who visit us, and we're grateful for it. Message us on WhatsApp, call, or just walk in and say hello.
