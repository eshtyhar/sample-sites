About us

About Park Physical Therapy

Park Physical Therapy PLLC is a physical therapy practice at 68-05 Main St in Queens, serving Flushing, Kew Gardens Hills and the surrounding neighbourhoods. Our focus is on quality of life: helping people feel more at ease in the everyday things they want to do.

Every visit is personal. We take time to listen, explain what we're doing and why, and work with you on a plan you understand. You can book online through Zocdoc or call the office to set up an appointment.

  • Listening firstEvery plan starts with understanding what matters to you.
  • Clear explanationsWe explain each step in plain words so you always know what's next.
  • Respect for your timeSimple booking and organised visits that fit into your week.
