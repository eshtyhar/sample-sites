About us

About Little Grace Bakery

We are a neighborhood cafe serving freshly made coffee, breakfast, and lunch in Midtown. Our bakery offers a variety of pastries, cakes, and cookies, perfect for a quick bite or a special event.

Little Grace Bakery is a neighborhood cafe and bakery located in Midtown, New York. We are dedicated to serving our community with high-quality coffee, freshly baked goods, and friendly service.

Our kitchen prepares a variety of items every day, from morning pastries to lunch sandwiches. We also specialize in custom cakes for birthdays, weddings, and other events.

We invite you to stop by for a coffee and a pastry, or to discuss a custom order for your next celebration.

  • QualityWe use the best ingredients for our food and drinks.
  • CommunityWe are proud to serve our neighbors in Midtown.
  • FreshnessEverything is made fresh daily in our kitchen.
