About us

An Italian table in the heart of Manhattan

We're a place to sit down for a proper Italian meal, grab something quick, or pick up ingredients to cook at home. Guests from Chelsea, Flatiron and all over the city have given us a 4.4 rating across more than 3,300 Google reviews. Message us on WhatsApp with any questions before you come in.

Eataly - Flatiron sits at 200 5th Ave, close to Chelsea and Flatiron, where the city's busiest streets meet. It's a place to eat, drink and shop for Italian food, all under one roof.

Some people come in for a quick espresso, some for a long dinner, and some to stock up for cooking at home. Either way, the idea is the same: good ingredients, prepared with care, served in a relaxed and welcoming room.

Planning a visit, a group meal or catering? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll help you sort out the details.
