About us

A neighbourhood diner on 9th Avenue

Westway Diner is at 614 9th Ave, a short walk from the theatres and busy streets of Midtown West. Come in for an early breakfast, a quick lunch or a late plate after a show. Guests rate us 4.4 on Google, and for any questions you can message us on WhatsApp.

Westway Diner sits at 614 9th Ave in Manhattan, where Midtown West meets the theatre district. It is the kind of place where you can slide into a booth, order what you feel like and take your time.

A good diner is all about familiar food done well, friendly service and a room that feels easy whether you come alone, with family or with a group. Breakfast, lunch or dinner, the aim is a satisfying meal that brings you back.
