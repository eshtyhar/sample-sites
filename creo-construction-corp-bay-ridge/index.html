About us

About Creo Construction Corp.

We're based on 68th Street, so we know Bay Ridge homes well, from limestone row houses to pre-war apartments and storefronts along the avenues. We plan carefully, keep you updated and leave a clean site. Message us on WhatsApp to get started.

Creo Construction Corp. is a Brooklyn construction company based at 316 68th St in Bay Ridge. We take on renovation, repair and building work for homeowners, landlords and local businesses in the neighbourhood and beyond.

Our approach is simple. We listen first, plan the work properly and then build with care. Bay Ridge buildings have real character, and we treat every project like it's on our own block.

If you're planning a project, big or small, message us on WhatsApp. Tell us what you have in mind and we'll take it from there.

  • Quality workmanshipCareful, solid work that holds up long after we've left.
  • Honest communicationStraight answers and regular updates from start to finish.
  • Respect for your spaceWe keep the site tidy and respect your home, your neighbours and your block.
