Cafes and bakeries

Old version. The remake (a real bakery inside) stopped halfway when the PC restarted.

THE COUNTER: an animated sample-site template for every New York coffee shop, bakery, bagel shop, juice bar and dessert or ice cream shop. One sunny Blender counter with a plain white bakery box, and the scroll is one order coming together: the flat box folds itself up, their own product photo drops into it, the lid shuts with twine and a seal (their logo), the boxes stack for big orders and it waits in the late sun, with Order ahead, Menu, Cakes & catering, Call and Directions on every screen. 15 desktop + 12 phone frames as a linked FigCraft prototype, one PDF, the renders in 3 moods with 3 scroll sequences, and build-spec.md.

These are the designer's screens (pictures), shown in scroll order. The real moving website is built only after you say yes.
