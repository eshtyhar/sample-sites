About us

About the Store

We are a local game store in Astoria dedicated to serving the community. Our shop provides a welcoming space for gamers of all ages to find the latest releases, explore a wide selection of pre-owned games and consoles, and connect with fellow enthusiasts.

Resorts World New York City is a comprehensive gaming destination located in the heart of Astoria. Our store is designed to be a hub for the local gaming community, offering a diverse range of products and services to meet the needs of every type of gamer.

Our inventory includes the latest video game releases for all major platforms, alongside a robust selection of pre-owned games and consoles. We also provide a range of accessories and collectibles for enthusiasts to enjoy.

Beyond our retail offerings, we are committed to fostering a sense of community. We host regular events and game nights, providing a space for friends and fellow gamers to connect and play together.

  • Customer SatisfactionWe are dedicated to providing a positive shopping experience for every customer.
  • CommunityWe believe in building a welcoming and inclusive space for all gamers.
  • ExpertiseOur staff is knowledgeable and passionate about games and gaming culture.
