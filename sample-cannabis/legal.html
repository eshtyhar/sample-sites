Accessibility, privacy and terms

Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use this site: it works with a keyboard and a screen reader, the words have strong contrast, and nothing moves if your device asks for less motion.

Something hard to use? Call us at (718) 555-0163 and we will help, and fix it.

Privacy

When you place an order or ask a question, we use your name, phone and email only to reply to you and to get your order ready. We never sell them or share them with anyone else.

This site remembers that you said you are 21 or older for 30 days, on your own device. It does not use tracking cookies. Ask us at (718) 555-0163 to see or delete what we hold about you.

Terms

This site and the shop are for adults 21 and over. We check a valid photo ID at the door and again at pickup.

An order is ready to pick up when we text you. Stock and prices come from our live menu and can change during the day: you pay at the counter, by cash or debit.
