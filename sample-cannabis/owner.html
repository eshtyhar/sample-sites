How this sitebrings people in.

In plain words: six ways it turns people looking on their phone into menu views, pickup orders, calls and walk-ins.

No numbers, no promises: what it does, and why that helps.

  1. 01

    Your menu does the selling

    Your live menu, with what's really in stock and your prices, is what people look for first. Here it's one tap from every screen.

  2. 02

    Orders before they walk in

    Order ahead for pickup turns browsing at home into a bag waiting at your counter: a quick, easy stop for them.

  3. 03

    More walk-ins from people nearby

    Open now, hours, the map and Directions sit on every screen. Someone looking for a dispensary near them sees you are open and how to get there.

  4. 04

    Trust for first-timers

    Your licence, the 21+ check and a plain "first time? just ask" help new customers feel sure about choosing you.

  5. 05

    Questions become visits

    Call and Ask us sit on every screen, so a question about the menu or pickup reaches your counter instead of going unanswered.

  6. 06

    Regulars come back

    Your Google reviews up front and a text club or loyalty sign-up (if you run one) bring people back for their next visit.
