About us

About The Escape Game Brooklyn

We are a local game store and escape room destination in Williamsburg, offering immersive experiences and a wide selection of games for players of all ages.

The Escape Game Brooklyn is a premier destination for interactive entertainment and gaming in the Williamsburg neighborhood. We combine the excitement of a game store with the thrill of immersive escape room adventures. Our goal is to provide a space where friends and family can connect, challenge themselves, and have fun.

We offer a carefully curated selection of new and used games, ensuring that there is something for every type of player. From the latest console releases to classic board games, our store is stocked to meet the needs of the local gaming community. We believe in the power of play to bring people together.

  • CommunityWe are proud to be a part of the Williamsburg community and look forward to serving you.
  • QualityWe maintain high standards for our escape rooms and our game inventory.
  • FunOur top priority is to ensure everyone who visits has a fun and memorable experience.
