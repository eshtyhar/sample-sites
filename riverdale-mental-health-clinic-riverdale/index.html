About us

A neighbourhood clinic for Riverdale

Reaching out can be the hardest part, so we try to make it simple. Our clinic in Riverdale offers a quiet, respectful place to talk with someone who listens. Message us on WhatsApp or call to ask about appointments.

Riverdale Mental Health Clinic is a local clinic on West 239th Street in the Bronx, serving people from Riverdale and nearby neighbourhoods.

We believe good care starts with feeling comfortable. That means a quiet setting, respectful conversations and appointments that give you time to talk.

If you are thinking about booking a visit, message us on WhatsApp or call us at +1 718-796-5300. We will help with the practical details, and anything personal can wait for your appointment.

  • RespectEveryone who comes through our door is treated with dignity.
  • PrivacyPersonal conversations belong in the appointment room, not in messages.
  • SteadinessConsistent, reliable care you can count on, visit after visit.
