About us

A Bronx bar for sports fans

Stan's Sports Bar sits at 836 River Ave in the Bronx, and it's built for watching the game. Come by on a big night or a quiet afternoon. Rated 4.6 on Google, so you'll be in good company.

Stan's Sports Bar is on River Ave in the Bronx. It's a place for people who love the game and like watching it together. Whether you follow every pitch or just want to soak up the atmosphere, there's room for you at the bar.

We keep it simple: good company, the game on and a friendly welcome. If you have a question about your visit or you're bringing a group, message us on WhatsApp and we'll get back to you.
