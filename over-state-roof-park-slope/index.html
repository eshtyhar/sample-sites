About us

About Over State Roof

Over State Roof is a roofing contractor based on St Marks Ave, working with brownstones, row houses and small buildings around Park Slope. We look at the problem carefully, explain what we find in plain terms and get the work done cleanly. Message us on WhatsApp to get started.

Over State Roof is a roofing contractor working out of 443 St Marks Ave in Brooklyn. We work on the homes and buildings of Park Slope and the surrounding neighbourhoods, from brownstone flat roofs to gutters, flashing and everything in between.

Brooklyn roofs take a lot of punishment: summer heat, winter freeze-thaw, heavy rain and decades of patch jobs. We take the time to understand what's really going on up there, explain it clearly and fix it properly so you aren't calling someone back next season.

If your roof is leaking, showing its age or you just want someone to take a look, message us on WhatsApp and we'll take it from there.

  • Honest assessmentsWe tell you what your roof needs, and what it doesn't.
  • Careful workClean installs, tidy sites and attention to the details that stop leaks.
  • Respect for your homeWe treat every building like it's on our own street.
