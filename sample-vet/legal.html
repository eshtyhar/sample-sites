Accessibility, privacy and terms

Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use this site: it works with a keyboard and a screen reader, the words have strong contrast, and nothing moves if your device asks for less motion.

Something hard to use? Call us at (718) 555-0172 and we will help, and fix it.

Privacy

When you send a request, we use your name, phone and email only to reply to you and to book your visit. We never sell them or share them with anyone else.

This site does not use tracking cookies. Ask us at (718) 555-0172 to see or delete what we hold about you.

Terms

A request is not a confirmed visit until we call or text you to confirm the time.

Prices and plans can change: we always tell you the price before we do anything.
