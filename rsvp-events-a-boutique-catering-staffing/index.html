About us

About RSVP Events

From an office lunch to an evening reception, we bring the food and the people who serve it. Planning is personal and the service is polished, so you can spend your time with your guests. Message us on WhatsApp with your date and guest count.

RSVP Events is a boutique catering and staffing company based at 135 Madison Avenue in Midtown Manhattan. We look after both the food and the people who serve it, so your event runs as one well-planned whole.

Good event catering comes from listening first. We want to know the occasion, the room, the guests and the feel you want, then we build the menu and the service around it. Our staff are there to make your guests comfortable and keep the day on track.

Whether it's an office gathering in Midtown, a private celebration at home or a reception at a venue across the city, the easiest way to start is to message us on WhatsApp.

  • Attentive serviceGuests should feel looked after from the first drink to the last plate.
  • Thoughtful foodMenus that fit the occasion and are prepared and presented with care.
  • Clear planningOpen communication so you always know what's happening and when.
