About us

Sushi and poke in Lower Manhattan

Sakara is a neighbourhood spot for sushi and poke, right on Broadway in Lower Manhattan. Whether you need a quick lunch between meetings or dinner on the way home, we make every roll and bowl with care. Message us on WhatsApp to order or ask a question.

Sakara Sushi & Poke is at 47 Broadway in Lower Manhattan, where people want lunch that's fresh, filling and quick to grab. We keep the menu focused on sushi and poke and try to do both well.

We make every order fresh, and we're happy to adjust a bowl or roll to suit you. Coming in from the office, a nearby hotel or the street? Message us on WhatsApp or call to order, and we'll take care of the rest.

  • Freshness firstWe make each roll and bowl to order.
  • Keep it simpleClean flavours and good ingredients, without overcomplicating the plate.
  • Neighbourly serviceWe're friendly and quick, and happy to help you choose.
