About us

A neighbourhood cafe in Greenpoint

We're a small cafe on Green Street in Greenpoint, Brooklyn, where people come for a good cup and a calm place to sit for a minute. Whether you want something fast on the way to work or a slow coffee with a friend, there's a seat for you. Questions? Message us on WhatsApp.

Coffee Check is a cafe at 65A Green St in Brooklyn. We believe a good coffee shop is simple: well-made drinks, a warm welcome and somewhere comfortable to take a short break from the day.

Greenpoint is a neighbourhood of early walks, quiet side streets and people who notice good coffee. We want to be the place you stop in on the way to work, meet a friend, or sit with a book for a while.

Our 4.8 rating on Google comes from the people who visit us, and we're grateful for every one of them. If you have a question or want to plan a visit, message us on WhatsApp.

  • Care in every cupEach drink is made to order and made properly, every time.
  • Warm hospitalityA friendly hello and a relaxed room, whether you stay or take it to go.
  • Rooted in GreenpointWe're proud to be part of the Green Street neighbourhood.
