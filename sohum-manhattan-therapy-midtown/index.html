About us

About Sōhum Manhattan Therapy

Our office sits in Suite 1005 at 171 Madison Ave, a quiet space above the pace of Midtown. Each visit is built around time, attention and clear communication. When you're ready, message us on WhatsApp and we'll help you find a time.

Sōhum Manhattan Therapy is a care practice based in Suite 1005 at 171 Madison Ave, in the middle of Midtown Manhattan. We set up our office as a calm, private space where people can slow down and focus on their visit.

Our approach is simple: listen carefully, explain clearly and treat every person with respect. We want each visit to feel considered and comfortable, from the first message to the follow-up.

If you'd like to know more or book a visit, message us on WhatsApp or call +1 646-397-4310.

  • AttentionEvery visit gets our full focus, without rushing.
  • RespectWe treat your time, privacy and choices with care.
  • ClarityPlain explanations, so you always know what to expect.
