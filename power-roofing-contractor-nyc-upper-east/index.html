About Power Roofing Contractor NYC

New York weather is hard on a roof, from summer heat to winter freeze and heavy rain. Power Roofing Contractor NYC helps building owners, co-op boards and homeowners keep the water out and the structure sound. Send a photo and a short note on WhatsApp and we'll talk you through the next step.

Power Roofing Contractor NYC is a roofing company serving the Upper East Side and the surrounding Manhattan neighbourhoods. We work on the roofs that keep the city's homes, townhouses and apartment buildings dry.

A good roof job starts with understanding the building. We look at how water moves across your roof, where the weak points are and what the structure needs, then we recommend work that fits the problem.

To get started, message us on WhatsApp with a few photos and your address, or call +1 718-810-4477. We'll get back to you about next steps.

  • Do it properlyWe fix the cause of a problem so you aren't calling about the same leak next season.
  • Respect the buildingWe treat every rooftop, stairwell and neighbour with care.
  • Keep you informedYou'll know what we're doing, why we're doing it and what comes next.
