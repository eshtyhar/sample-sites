About us

An Indian kitchen in Jackson Heights

You'll find us at 37-40B 74th St., on one of the liveliest food streets in Queens. We cook Indian food with plenty of spice, slow-simmered sauces and the kind of hospitality that brings people back. Come in for a meal, bring the family, or message us on WhatsApp to order ahead.

Jackson Diner is an Indian restaurant at 37-40B 74th St., in the part of Jackson Heights where spice shops, sweet shops and sari stores share the block. It's a place to sit down for a proper meal, whether that's a quick lunch or a long dinner with the family.

Our cooking is built on what makes Indian food so satisfying: whole spices toasted until fragrant, sauces simmered slowly, and bread made fresh so it reaches the table warm. You'll find dishes for meat lovers and plenty for vegetarians.

To ask about the menu, order takeout or plan a meal for a group, message us on WhatsApp. We're happy to help.
