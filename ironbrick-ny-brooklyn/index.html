About us

About Ironbrick NY

Ironbrick NY works out of Stratford Road in Brooklyn and takes on the kind of projects that keep Brooklyn homes and buildings in good shape. We communicate clearly from the first conversation to the final walkthrough. Message us on WhatsApp to talk through your plans.

Ironbrick NY is a construction business on Stratford Road in Brooklyn. We work on the homes and buildings that make up the borough, from small repairs to full renovations.

We think good construction starts with listening. We find out what you need, explain how we'd approach it, and then do the work carefully, keeping you updated as it goes.

If you have a project in mind, or just a question about one, message us on WhatsApp. We're glad to talk it through.

  • Solid workmanshipWork done properly, built to last in a New York climate.
  • Honest conversationsStraight answers about what your project needs.
  • Respect for your spaceWe treat your home and street with care.
