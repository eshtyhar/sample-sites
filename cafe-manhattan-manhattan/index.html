About us

A simple cafe in the heart of Manhattan

Cafe Manhattan is a neighbourhood cafe at 35 W 45th St, right in the middle of Manhattan's busy weekdays. We keep it simple: good coffee, fresh food and quick, friendly service. Message us on WhatsApp to ask about today's menu or to order ahead.

Cafe Manhattan sits at 35 W 45th St, a short walk from offices, theatres and the busy streets of Midtown. It's the kind of place people come back to for their morning coffee, a quick lunch or a short break in a long day.

We keep things straightforward: coffee made with care, fresh food and a welcoming counter. Whether you're grabbing something to go or sitting down for a few minutes, we want it to be easy.

Have a question about the menu or want to order ahead? Message us on WhatsApp or give us a call at +1 212-921-1515.
