About us

About Our Practice

We provide a supportive environment for mental well-being. Our team offers consistent, professional care for individuals and families in the Upper East Side.

Manhattan Psychotherapy Services LCSW, PLLC is located on the Upper East Side of Manhattan. We are dedicated to providing accessible mental health care to our community.

Our practice focuses on creating a calm and professional atmosphere. We work with clients to understand their needs and support their journey toward well-being.

We offer a variety of services to meet different needs, including individual, couples, and family therapy. We also provide telehealth options for those who prefer to meet remotely.

  • CompassionTreating every client with kindness and understanding.
  • ConfidentialityEnsuring a safe and private space for all discussions.
  • ProfessionalismProviding ethical and skilled care to all patients.
