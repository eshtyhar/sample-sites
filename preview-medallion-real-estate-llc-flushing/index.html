About us

About Medallion

Medallion Real Estate LLC is a property management company based at 112-18 Queens Blvd in Queens. We offer full service management under one roof for owners across the NY area.

Our work is simple to describe: keep buildings running well, keep residents looked after and keep owners informed. Our online payment and resident portal helps with all three.

If you own a building and want a management partner close by, request a proposal or give our office a call.

  • ResponsivenessQuestions from owners and residents get a reply and a clear next step.
  • TransparencyOwners can see what's happening with their building and their money.
  • Care for the buildingWe treat each property as a long-term asset worth looking after properly.
