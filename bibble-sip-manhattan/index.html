About us

A little cafe with a sweet side

Just off the bustle of Midtown, at 253 W 51st St, we make carefully brewed coffee and sweet treats worth stopping for. Come by before a show, between meetings or on a slow afternoon. Our 4.5 rating on Google comes from guests who keep coming back.

Bibble & Sip is a Midtown Manhattan cafe on West 51st Street, built around two simple pleasures: a good drink and something sweet to go with it.

We want it to be a place where you can step out of the rush, whether you have five minutes or an hour. Order to go or stay a while, and leave feeling a bit better than when you walked in.

Have a question about the menu, a larger order or your visit? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll help you out.

  • Care in every cupDrinks made properly, one at a time.
  • Fresh and sweetBakes that are a real treat, not an afterthought.
  • Warm welcomeFriendly service for regulars and first-timers alike.
