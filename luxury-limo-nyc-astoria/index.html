About us

About Luxury Limo NYC

We help Queens and New York riders get where they're going comfortably and on time. Whether it's a flight, a wedding or a night out, tell us your plans and we'll help you pick the right car. Message us on WhatsApp to get started.

Luxury Limo NYC is a chauffeured car and limo service based in Queens, with riders across Astoria and the wider city. We focus on smooth trips: clean cars, clear plans and a driver who turns up when expected.

Every ride is different. Some people need a quick airport transfer, others want a whole evening planned around a celebration. We listen to what you need and help you pick the car that fits, so you can relax and enjoy the trip.

Our 4.9 Google rating comes from riders who trusted us with their important days. To check availability for your date, message us on WhatsApp.

  • On timeWe plan around your schedule so you're never left waiting.
  • Comfort firstClean, comfortable cars and a calm, easy ride.
  • Clear communicationStraight answers about your booking from first message to drop-off.
