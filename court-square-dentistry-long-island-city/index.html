About us

A neighbourhood practice in Long Island City

We're a neighbourhood dental practice at 24-24 Jackson Ave, a short walk from Court Square. Whether you live in Long Island City or work nearby, we make it simple to keep up with your dental visits. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll help you find a time.

Court Square Dentistry is a dental practice at 24-24 Jackson Ave, in the heart of Long Island City. We look after patients from the neighbourhood's towers and townhouses, and from offices around Court Square, with straightforward, friendly care.

We think a dental visit should feel calm and clear. That means listening first, explaining things simply and making it easy to keep up with regular care for you and your family.

To book a visit or ask a general question, message us on WhatsApp or call us at +1 718-392-0646.

  • ComfortA calm, welcoming visit from the moment you walk in.
  • ClarityHonest, plain-language conversations about your care.
  • CommunityProud to be part of the Long Island City neighbourhood.
