About us

A sweet stop in Greenwich Village

Empire Cake is a neighbourhood cafe at 450 6th Ave, right in the heart of Greenwich Village. Come in for a slice and a coffee, pick up something sweet on the way home, or plan a cake for your next celebration. Guests rate us 4.7 on Google, and we'd love to welcome you next.

Empire Cake is a cafe at 450 6th Ave, in one of New York's best-loved neighbourhoods. The Village is full of people who walk, browse and linger, and we're here for every one of them, from a quick coffee to a box of cake for later.

We keep things simple: good cake, good coffee and a friendly welcome at the counter. Whether you stop by for one slice or plan a cake for a special day, we want it to feel easy and worth it.
