Celebration cakes

A cake made just for your day

We bake custom celebration cakes to order. Please get in touch at least 3 days before you need it.

  1. Email us or stop by the bakery, at least 3 days ahead.
  2. Tell us your date and what you are celebrating.
  3. Collect your cake at 214 Court Street.
