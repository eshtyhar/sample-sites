About us

Two sisters and a neighbourhood oven

Maple & Rye is a small bakery on Court Street in Brooklyn. We are two sisters, and we bake for our neighbours every morning.

Baked fresh, every morning

Each morning we bake naturally leavened sourdough loaves, butter croissants, seasonal fruit tarts and cinnamon buns. When the doors open at 7am, they are ready on the counter.

We pour drip coffee and make espresso drinks too, so you can pick up a cup with your bake.

And when there is something to celebrate, we bake custom cakes to order.

What you will find here

Bread and pastries

Sourdough, croissants, fruit tarts and cinnamon buns, baked every morning.

Celebration cakes

Custom cakes to order. Please ask at least 3 days ahead.

Coffee

Drip coffee and espresso drinks to go with your bake.
