How to order a cake

We bake custom celebration cakes to order. We need at least 3 days' notice, so get in touch early.

  1. Email us or stop by the bakery, at least 3 days before you need the cake.
  2. Tell us the date and what you are celebrating.
  3. Collect your cake at 214 Court Street, Tuesday to Sunday between 7am and 3pm.
