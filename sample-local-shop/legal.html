Accessibility, privacy and terms

Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use this site: it works with a keyboard and a screen reader, the words have strong contrast, and nothing moves if your device asks for less motion.

The shop is at street level with a step-free entrance. Something hard to use, on the site or in the shop? Call us at (718) 555-0147 and we will help.

Privacy

When you ask about an item, we use your name and mobile only to answer you and, if you ask, to hold it for you. We never sell them or share them with anyone else.

This site does not use tracking cookies. Ask us at (718) 555-0147 to see or delete what we hold about you.

Terms

An answer about stock is a quick check of the shelf, not a reservation: an item is held for you only when we text to say so. We hold it for up to 2 days, free.

Prices are as marked in the shop and can change. Returns and exchanges within 14 days with the receipt.
