About us

Coffee, roasted and served with attention

We're a coffee roaster and cafe on the west side of Manhattan, focused on good beans and a cup made properly. Whether you're on your way to work or settling in for a while, there's something here for you. Questions or orders? Message us on WhatsApp.

Afficionado Coffee Roasters is a coffee roaster and cafe at 750 11th Avenue in Manhattan. We care about the whole journey of the bean, from roasting to the moment it reaches your cup.

Our cafe is a place for a reliable morning coffee, a quiet break in the afternoon, or picking up beans to brew at home. We keep things simple: good coffee, made properly, served with a friendly welcome.

Have a question about our coffee, beans or an order? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll get back to you.
