Accessibility, privacy and terms

Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use this site: it works with a keyboard and a screen reader, the words have strong contrast, and nothing moves if your device asks for less motion.

The shop has a step-free entrance from the street. Something hard to use, on the site or in the shop? Call us at (718) 555-0148 and we will help.

Privacy

When you ask us to check stock, we use your name, mobile and email only to answer you. We never sell them or share them with anyone else. Your answer to the 21+ question stays on your own device for 30 days.

This site does not use tracking cookies. Ask us at (718) 555-0148 to see or delete what we hold about you.

Terms

Everything we sell is for adults 21 and over. We check a valid photo ID at the counter for every sale, and we can't sell without one.

A stock answer is a quick check of the shelf, not a reservation: we hold an item for you only when we text to say so. We don't ship or deliver: pick up in store. Prices are as marked at the counter and can change.
