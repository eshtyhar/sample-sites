How this sitebrings people in.

In plain words: six ways it turns people looking on their phone into stock questions, calls and walk-ins.

No numbers, no promises: what it does, and why that helps.

  1. 01

    More walk-ins from people nearby

    Open now, hours, the map and Directions sit on every screen. Someone looking for a smoke shop near them at night sees you are open and how to get there.

  2. 02

    Stock questions turn into visits

    “Check stock” is one tap away. People ask before they come, you answer by text, and they walk in sure it's on the shelf.

  3. 03

    Your range, seen before the visit

    Your categories with your own shelf photos show what you carry, so people come to you instead of the shop with no website.

  4. 04

    Your late hours work for you

    Your real hours, open now and last sale, always right. Late-night customers don't guess, they come.

  5. 05

    Adults only, said up front

    A 21+ check opens the site and every page says to bring ID. The right customers come in, ready at the counter.

  6. 06

    Regulars come back

    A text club for new arrivals and your Google reviews up front. People who liked the shop hear when something new is in.
