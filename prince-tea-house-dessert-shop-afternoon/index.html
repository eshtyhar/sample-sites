About us

A tea room for Flushing

Just off the busy streets of downtown Flushing, we make room for an unhurried afternoon. Come for a full tea service, a slice of cake with a friend, or a quiet cup on your own. Guests have given us a 4.3 rating on Google, and we'd be glad to have you at our table.

Prince Tea House is a dessert shop and afternoon tea room at 36-39 Prince St in Flushing. We set out to make a place where an afternoon can stretch out a little, with good tea, something sweet and a comfortable seat.

Flushing moves fast, and we like being the place where people pause. Friends catch up here, families celebrate here, and some guests just come in to read with a pot of tea. Everyone gets the same welcome.

Want to plan a visit, check what's on the table today or set up a small gathering? Message us on WhatsApp or call +1 917-285-2523.

  • UnhurriedTea tastes better when nobody's rushing you, so we never do.
  • Made with careFrom the pour to the plate, we pay attention to the small details.
  • NeighborlyWe're a Flushing tea room, and everyone who comes in is welcome to stay a while.
