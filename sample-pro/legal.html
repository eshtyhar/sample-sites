Accessibility, privacy and terms

Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use this site: it works with a keyboard and a screen reader, the words have strong contrast, and nothing moves if your device asks for less motion.

The office is in Suite 2B: Elevator to the 2nd floor, step-free from the street. Something hard to use, on the site or in the building? Call us at (718) 555-0147 and we will help.

Privacy

When you book a consultation or send documents, we use your name, phone and email only to call you back, set the time and get ready for the meeting. We never sell them or share them with anyone else.

Please don't write the details of your case in the forms: we talk about them in the meeting. This site does not use tracking cookies. Ask us at (718) 555-0147 to see or delete what we hold about you.

Terms

Everything on this site is general information only, not legal or tax advice. Writing to us, or booking a consultation, doesn't make you a client: that starts only when we both sign a written agreement.

A consultation request is not a booked time until we call you to confirm it. Fees are always set out in writing before any work starts.
