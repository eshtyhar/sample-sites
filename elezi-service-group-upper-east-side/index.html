About Elezi Service Group

We work from East 77th Street, so the Upper East Side is our home ground. That means we know prewar apartments, co-op rules and tight Manhattan job sites. Whatever you're planning, message us on WhatsApp and we'll talk it through with you.

Elezi Service Group is a construction company based at 428 East 77th Street on Manhattan's Upper East Side. We take on renovation, remodeling and general construction for apartments, townhouses and commercial spaces.

Building in Manhattan has its own challenges: freight elevator schedules, building approvals, neighbours on every side and very little room to work. We plan around all of it so your project runs smoothly and the result holds up.

Got a project in mind? Message us on WhatsApp or call +1 347-345-7860 and we'll talk through what you need.

  • Careful workMeasured twice, built properly, finished cleanly.
  • Honest communicationStraight answers about scope, timing and next steps.
  • Respect for your homeWe treat your space and your building with care every day we're there.
