About us

A corner deli for Flatbush

Ortiz Deli & Market is a neighbourhood stop at 1606 Nostrand Ave for a quick breakfast, a solid lunch or the groceries you forgot. Neighbours rate us 4.5 stars on Google. Message us on WhatsApp to place an order or check what's fresh.

Ortiz Deli & Market sits at 1606 Nostrand Ave, part of the everyday rhythm of Flatbush. People stop in on the way to work, on a lunch break or on the way home, and we try to make each visit quick and easy.

Behind the counter, the idea is simple. Sandwiches are made to order, the coffee is fresh, and the shelves carry the basics you need during the week. Nothing fancy, just good deli food done properly.

Want to order ahead, ask about a tray for a gathering, or check whether something is in stock? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll help you out.
