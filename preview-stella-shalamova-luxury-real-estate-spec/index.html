About us

About Stella Shalamova

Stella Shalamova is a luxury real estate specialist based on Union Turnpike, serving Flushing and the neighbouring parts of Queens. Her approach is simple: elite service you deserve, whether you are buying, selling or renting.

Every client gets her personal attention, honest advice on the market and help at each step, from the first viewing or valuation through to closing. She also works with trusted business partners for mortgages and pre-approval, so the process feels joined up.

If you're thinking about a move, the easiest first step is a quick message on WhatsApp or a call.

  • Personal attentionYour goals set the plan, and you always know who to call.
  • HonestyStraight advice on pricing and the market, even when it isn't what you hoped to hear.
  • Fair and open to allEvery buyer, seller and renter is welcomed and treated with the same care.

Ask on WhatsApp
How do I find out what my home is worth?

Use the home value request or message Stella on WhatsApp with your address and a few details, and she'll get back to you to discuss it.

Should I get pre-approved before I start looking?

It usually helps you know your budget and move quickly on the right home. Stella can introduce you to lending partners; just call or message on WhatsApp.

Do you help with rentals as well as sales?

Yes. Stella works with people looking to rent and with owners looking to lease. Message on WhatsApp to tell her what you need.

Which areas do you cover?

Flushing and nearby Queens neighbourhoods such as Fresh Meadows, Jamaica Estates and Bayside. If you're unsure about your area, just call to ask.

How do I book a viewing or attend an open house?

Check the open houses section or message Stella on WhatsApp with the home you're interested in to arrange a time.
