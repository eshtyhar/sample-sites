About us

A Harlem roofing company

We're a roofing and construction company based right here in Harlem, working on the rowhouses, brownstones and apartment buildings that make this neighbourhood what it is. Whether it's a leak after a storm or a full roof replacement, we look at the problem properly and tell you plainly what it needs. Message us on WhatsApp to get started.

NYC Supreme Roofing Construction Corp is a roofing and construction company based at 561 W 141st St in Harlem. We work on the kinds of buildings you see on every uptown block, from brownstones and rowhouses to larger apartment buildings.

New York roofs take a lot of punishment from summer heat, heavy rain, snow and freeze-thaw cycles. A good roofer finds where the problem actually starts, fixes it properly, and tells you honestly whether you need a repair or a replacement. That's how we approach every job.

If you're dealing with a leak, a worn-out roof or you just want to know what shape your roof is in, message us on WhatsApp and we'll take it from there.

  • Do it properlyWe fix the cause of a problem, not just what you can see.
  • Be clearYou'll know what we're doing, why we're doing it, and what comes next.
  • Respect the buildingWe treat your home or building, and your neighbours, with care while we work.
