About us

A small Italian kitchen in Brooklyn

We cook the way Italian families do at home: simple, generous and made with care. Find us at 141 Nevins St, a short walk from the heart of Park Slope. To book a table or ask a question, message us on WhatsApp.

Piccola Cucina Casa means 'little kitchen, home', and that is the feeling we cook for. The food is straightforward and generous, the kind of Italian cooking that's meant for a full table and a long conversation.

We're at 141 Nevins St, Brooklyn, serving neighbours from Park Slope and nearby. Some people come in for a quick plate of pasta, others for a slow dinner with family. Either way, you'll be welcome.

Want to book a table, order to take away or ask about the menu? Message us on WhatsApp or call +1 929-295-0015.

  • SimplicityA few good ingredients, cooked well, so every plate tastes like itself.
  • HospitalityEvery guest is treated like someone who's eating at our own table.
  • CareEach dish is made with attention, whether you eat in or take it home.
