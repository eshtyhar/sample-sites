About us

About Us

We provide a calm, professional environment for individuals and families seeking support. Our focus is on creating a safe space for personal growth and well-being.

Union Square Psychology is a clinic in Midtown dedicated to providing care for the whole family. We offer a range of services to support individuals, couples, and families in their journey toward well-being.

Our clinic provides a calm, professional environment where you can feel safe to share and grow. We focus on creating a supportive atmosphere that helps you navigate life's challenges.

We are committed to offering guidance and support through various therapeutic approaches. Our goal is to help you find balance and work towards your personal goals.

  • CompassionWe approach every person with kindness and understanding.
  • ConfidentialityYour privacy is our highest priority in every session.
  • ProfessionalismWe provide expert care in a respectful and ethical manner.
